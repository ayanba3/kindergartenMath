The little challenge

Can you measure four liters?

You have a 3-liter jug and a 5-liter jug, plus a tap and a sink. Neither jug has markings. Can you end with exactly 4 liters in the 5-liter jug?

01Allowed moves: fill a jug, empty a jug, or pour until one jug is full or the other is empty.
02No guessing partial pours. Each move follows the jug shape.
1 Choose a legal move
3 L jug

Start with any legal move. Goal: exactly 4 L in the 5 L jug.

Moves: 0 · State history (3 L, 5 L): (0,0).

Why does this work?
In the order (3 L, 5 L), the states are (0,0) → (0,5) → (3,2) → (0,2) → (2,0) → (2,5) → (3,4). Four liters remain in the 5 L jug. Each transition is legal because every pour stops when a jug reaches empty or full.
