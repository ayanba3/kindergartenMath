Combinatorics · Rotational symmetry

Rotation detectives.

Four beads, two colors: how many different necklaces? Turn them, but do not flip them.

Turn, don’t flip

Four positions go around a necklace. T is teal with dots; C is coral with stripes. A clockwise turn keeps the same necklace, but the beads are not flipped over.

Numbers mark inspection slots: 1 top, then clockwise. Letter and pattern show each bead’s color.

Change two beads or turn once, then sort all 16.

Why sorting works

Every fixed-slot string has four possible rotations. Sorting strings by their smallest rotation groups exactly the views of one necklace. Some necklaces have fewer than four distinct views, so dividing 16 by 4 would be wrong.

Total tickets: 24 · 16 + 2 + 4 + 2 = 24.

Why divide 24 tickets by 4?

In any family with m different views, each view is unchanged by 4/m turns. So its views make m × (4/m) = 4 tickets. Every family contributes exactly 4 tickets. We counted 24 tickets, so there must be 24 ÷ 4 = 6 families.
