Your discovery

Count each pair just once.

5 friends × 4 other friends = 20 greetings in the table.
Every handshake has two entries, so 20 ÷ 2 = 10 handshakes.

H(n) = n(n − 1) ÷ 2

H(n) is the number of handshakes for n friends. We multiply by n − 1 because nobody greets themselves. We divide by 2 because each pair appears twice.

3 One more friend joins…
Another way to count

Let A greet 4 others. B now has 3 new people to greet, C has 2, and D has 1. E’s greetings are already counted.

4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 10. When a sixth friend joins, add 5: 10 + 5 = 15.
