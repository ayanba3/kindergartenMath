Why does this work?
The 12 edge-middle cubelets have exactly two painted faces: a 3×3 cube has 12 edges, and each edge contributes one middle cubelet. The 8 corner cubelets have three painted faces. The 6 face-center cubelets have one painted face. The single center cubelet has no painted faces. The visible top layer is a true 3×3 slice: its four corners are 3-face pieces, its four edge middles are 2-face pieces, and its center has 1 face.
