Your challenge

A number N is between 19 and 99. The distance from 19 to N is the same as the distance from N to 99. What is N?

Hint: think about the whole distance

There are 80 steps from 19 to 99. If N splits that distance into two equal parts, how many steps belong on each side? Start at 19 and move that many steps.

Show the reasoning

Split the whole distance in half: 80 ÷ 2 = 40. Move 40 steps from 19: 19 + 40 = 59. Check both sides: 59 − 19 = 40 and 99 − 59 = 40. So N = 59.

The middle of two numbers is equally far from both endpoints.
