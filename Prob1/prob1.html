Why is 59 the sweet spot?

The purple path and orange path meet in the middle. If Pip moves, one gets longer while the other gets shorter.

80 ÷ 2 = 40

The whole walk from 19 to 99 is 80 steps. Sharing it equally gives 40 steps on each side. Start at 19, then walk 40: 19 + 40 = 59.
