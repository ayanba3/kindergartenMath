The little challenge

Where should Pip stop?

A path starts at 19 and ends at 99. Pip wants to stop at a number, N, that is equally far from both ends.

What number should Pip choose?

A little nudge ↗

The whole path is 99 − 19 = 80 steps long. What happens if you share those steps equally between the two sides?

Why does it work?

Half of 80 is 40. Start at 19 and take 40 steps: 19 + 40 = 59. There are also 40 steps from 59 to 99. That’s why N = 59 is right in the middle.
