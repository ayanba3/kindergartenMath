Which coin is heavier?

Why does this work?
Split the eight coins into groups of 3, 3, and 2. Weigh the two groups of three. If one side dips, the heavy coin is among those three; compare two of them, and if they balance, the third is heavy. If the first weighing balances, the heavy coin is one of the two set aside; compare those two. Either way, two weighings identify it.
