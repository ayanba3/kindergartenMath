Think backward

Leave 11 so the next player must give you 12, 13, or 14. Then leave 7, then 3. Start by adding 3; when the opponent adds a, add 4−a, so your paired moves total 4 and land on the next stepping stone. From 12, 13, or 14, the next player can finish at 15, so leaving 11 is safe for the player who just moved. The same reasoning works backward for 7 and 3.

3, 7, and 11 are losing positions for the player about to move under perfect play.
