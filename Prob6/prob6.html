4 An output you can’t make?

Inputs must be non-negative whole numbers. Can this machine output 24?

24 − 2 = 22
1 counter left over

After removing two extras, 22 counters cannot fill three equal whole-number groups: 7 + 7 + 7 leaves 1 over.

Input = (output − 2) ÷ 3

For 24, this gives 22 ÷ 3 = 7⅓, which isn’t an allowed input.

For non-negative whole-number inputs, possible outputs are 2, 5, 8, 11, …: exactly the numbers at least 2 that leave remainder 2 when divided by 3.
