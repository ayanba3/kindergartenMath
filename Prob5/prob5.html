The little challenge

Make room to grow.

Pip has 24 pieces of fence, each 1 metre long. Use them all to make a rectangular garden.

Each 1 m × 1 m square holds one flower. Which rectangle holds the most flowers?

3 Build the two rules

Exactly: w + h = 12, so h = 12 − w.

Each row has w squares, and there are h rows. Multiply to count the spaces inside.

Area = w × h = w(12 − w)

Perimeter measures the boundary in metres. Area measures the space inside in square metres.

Why does balance win?

Start with the 6 × 6 square: 36 spaces. Make one side d metres longer and the other d metres shorter. The fence still measures 24 m.

(6 + d)(6 − d) = 36 − d²

You lose d² spaces whenever the sides move away from balance. At 5 × 7, d = 1, so you lose 1 space. At 4 × 8, d = 2, so you lose 4 spaces.

Among these rectangles, the square holds the most.
