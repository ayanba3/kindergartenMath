Why add instead of multiply?

Every lineup has exactly one Lead, so the Lead cases never overlap. Count the Guitar choices for each Drums branch, then add the branches. Finally add all the Lead cases: 4 + 10 + 0 + 4 + 7 = 25.

Some branches have fewer choices because Lily and Oscar cannot be together. That’s why counting the visible paths is useful.
